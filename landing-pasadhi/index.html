The order of things

Free first, rental second, on-prem always

We sell nothing until there is spare capacity. The sequence is deliberate: grow the pool with people who share, then rent the surplus, and always let an organisation buy the software outright.

Products, in order of release

AI Agent → Cloud → VPS

Each product is the same compute plane with a different face. Nothing is announced before it works.

A local agent that reads documents, answers questions and runs tools — on your own machines, with your own model, with the data never leaving the building.

Questions

What people ask before installing

Is it really free?

Yes, while the pool is being built: share 20–30 % of a machine and use AI agents at no charge. Paid rental starts only when the network has spare capacity.

What happens to my files?

The agent runs on your machines. In on-prem mode nothing leaves your network; in shared mode only encrypted job tiles are sent, and the worker that runs them never sees your files.

How much of my computer is used?

Whatever you set: the sliders cap CPU, GPU, RAM, disk and network. The default is 20–30 %, which leaves the machine responsive.

Do I need a GPU?

No. A CPU pool works; a small card such as a GTX 1050 makes a machine roughly 22× faster for the same job.

Can I stop sharing?

Yes, at any time, from the same window. The worker stops within one scheduling window and your credits stay in the account.
